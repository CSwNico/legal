Daten auf deinem Gerät

Kuniswy speichert deine Favoriten, deinen Lernfortschritt, deinen Suchverlauf und deine Einstellungen lokal auf deinem Gerät. Diese Daten werden nicht an mich übertragen.

Synchronisierung über iCloud

Wenn du auf deinem Gerät bei iCloud angemeldet bist, synchronisiert Kuniswy die oben genannten Daten über Apples CloudKit in die private Datenbank deines iCloud-Accounts, damit sie auf all deinen Geräten verfügbar sind. Die Daten werden von Apple Inc. bzw. Apple Distribution International Ltd. verarbeitet und sind nur für dich sichtbar. Ich als Entwickler habe keinen Zugriff darauf. Für die Synchronisierung verwendet das System stille Push-Benachrichtigungen; du erhältst dadurch keine sichtbaren Mitteilungen.

Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO (Bereitstellung der von dir gewünschten Funktion). Du kannst die Synchronisierung jederzeit in den iOS-Einstellungen unter „Apple-Account › iCloud“ deaktivieren. Weitere Informationen: Datenschutzrichtlinie von Apple.

YouTube-Videos

Kuniswy zeigt Tutorial-Videos von YouTube an. Anbieter ist Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland. Sobald Vorschaubilder geladen werden oder du ein Video öffnest, wird eine Verbindung zu den Servern von YouTube bzw. Google hergestellt. Dabei werden insbesondere deine IP-Adresse, Geräte- und Browserinformationen sowie das angesehene Video übermittelt. YouTube kann im eingebetteten Player Cookies oder ähnliche Technologien verwenden und die Daten auch in den USA verarbeiten. Bist du bei YouTube angemeldet, kann YouTube die Wiedergabe deinem Konto zuordnen.

Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse an einer ansprechenden Darstellung der Lerninhalte). Google ist unter dem EU-US Data Privacy Framework zertifiziert. Weitere Informationen: Datenschutzerklärung von Google.

Data on your device

Kuniswy stores your favorites, learning progress, search history and settings locally on your device. This data is not sent to me.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud on your device, Kuniswy syncs this data through Apple's CloudKit into the private database of your iCloud account so it's available on all your devices. The data is processed by Apple Inc. or Apple Distribution International Ltd. and is visible only to you. As the developer, I have no access to it. The system uses silent push notifications for syncing; you won't see any notifications because of this.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the feature you asked for). You can turn off syncing at any time in iOS Settings under "Apple Account › iCloud". More information: Apple Privacy Policy.

YouTube videos

Kuniswy shows tutorial videos from YouTube, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. As soon as thumbnails load or you open a video, your device connects to YouTube/Google servers. This sends in particular your IP address, device and browser information and the video you're watching. YouTube may use cookies or similar technologies in the embedded player and may also process the data in the USA. If you're signed in to YouTube, it may link the playback to your account.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in presenting the learning content well). Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More information: Google Privacy Policy.

Sample content in tutorials

Some code examples load data from public services to illustrate how things work, for example sample images from picsum.photos or sample data from the PokéAPI. This technically sends your IP address to that provider. No other data is sent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in working examples). Map examples use Apple Maps; your location is not requested for them.

Exercise mentor with Apple Intelligence

The exercise mentor uses Apple's Foundation Models framework. It runs entirely on your device. Your input is not sent to me or to anyone else.

App Store purchases

Kuniswy Pro is offered as a subscription or one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. I never receive payment details such as card or account numbers. Apple's Privacy Policy applies.

RevenueCat

To manage purchases and subscriptions I use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA). RevenueCat verifies your purchases with Apple, unlocks Kuniswy Pro and lets you restore purchases.

This mainly involves:

  • a randomly generated, anonymous user ID (no name, no email address),
  • purchase information such as product, price, currency, purchase and expiration date, and Apple transaction IDs,
  • technical data such as device model, operating system and app version, language, App Store country and IP address.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the purchase contract) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in reliable purchase handling and fraud prevention). The data is processed in the USA. The transfer is based on appropriate safeguards under Art. 46 GDPR (EU Standard Contractual Clauses). Data is kept as long as needed to manage your purchases or as required by law. More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
